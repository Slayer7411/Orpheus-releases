◆ WHAT IT DOES

  • local files and your streaming accounts in one ui: google drive, spotify, apple music, soundcloud, tidal
  • one queue, mixed playlists across all sources, history
  • move playlists between services: matched by isrc, then by title, you confirm unsure matches
  • airplay and sonos output
  • reads the 741 library: waveforms, cues and grids like in MINOS

streaming needs your own account at each service.
